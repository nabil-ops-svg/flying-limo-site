Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

How do I make a reservation?

Fill out the quote form below with your pickup, drop-off, date and time. We'll confirm your chauffeur and vehicle, usually within minutes.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major debit and credit cards. Payment is handled securely once your booking is confirmed.

What types of vehicles are available?

Our fleet includes executive sedans, premium SUVs like the Cadillac Escalade and GMC Yukon, and the Mercedes S580 for a fully chauffeured experience.

Are your vehicles and drivers insured?

Yes. Every vehicle is fully insured, and every chauffeur is trained and background-checked before joining our team.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in the Bay Area and serve San Francisco and surrounding areas. Contact us for availability outside that range.

Can you accommodate special requests?

Absolutely — child seats, extra stops, or specific vehicle preferences. Just note it in your booking and we'll take care of the rest.
